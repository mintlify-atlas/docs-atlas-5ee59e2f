vLLM Ascend Plugin is an open-source project under the [vllm-project](https://github.com/vllm-project) organization, community-maintained and governed according to a hierarchical technical structure. Its mission is to provide easy, fast, and cost-effective LLM serving for everyone on Ascend NPUs, while actively contributing to the broader vLLM ecosystem.

## Project structure

vLLM Ascend is a hardware plugin that lives under the vLLM community. The vLLM community holds ultimate authority to appoint roles and make final decisions, while day-to-day technical direction is driven by a group of maintainers. All governance decisions are made in the open through GitHub issues, pull requests, and weekly community meetings.

## Roles and responsibilities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Contributor">
    Contributors are community members who consistently and actively participate in the project. This includes — but is not limited to — filing issues, reviewing pull requests, submitting commits, and engaging with the community.

    **Requirements:** Complete at least one contribution to the project.

    **Responsibilities:**
    - Help new contributors get started
    - Handle and respond to community questions
    - Review RFCs and code changes

    Contributors are granted `Triage` permissions on the [vllm-project/vllm-ascend](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend) repository, giving them read and clone access along with the ability to manage issues and pull requests.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Maintainer">
    Maintainers are responsible for the project's technical direction and long-term success. They hold code merge permissions and lead roadmap planning.

    **Requirements:**
    - Deep understanding of both vLLM and vLLM Ascend codebases
    - Sustained, high-quality code contributions
    - Competency in design, development, and PR review workflows

    **Responsibilities:**
    - **Review quality:** Actively participate in community code reviews to ensure high-quality integration
    - **Quality contributions:** Successfully develop and deliver at least one major feature
    - **Community involvement:** Address issues, respond to forum inquiries, participate in discussions, and engage in community events such as weekly meetings

    Maintainers are granted write access to the [vllm-project/vllm-ascend](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend) repository, including permission to read, clone, push, and manage issues and pull requests.

    Approval from existing maintainers is required for new maintainer nominations. The vLLM community has final decision-making authority.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Nominating and removing maintainers

Membership in vLLM Ascend is merit-based. To be considered for the maintainer group, you must demonstrate strong and continued alignment with the overall vLLM Ascend principles through contributions, reviews, and discussions. Membership is granted to individuals, not companies.

**Nomination:** Anyone can nominate a candidate — including self-nominations. All existing maintainers review and evaluate each nomination. The nominator should provide evidence of the nominee's qualifications, such as review quality, contributions, and community involvement.

**Removal:** Anyone can nominate an individual for removal, including self-nominations. Maintainers evaluate removal nominations based on factors such as prolonged inactivity, misalignment with project direction, or other disqualifying factors.

**Emeritus status:** Maintainers who have been inactive for a long time may be transitioned to emeritus status under lenient criteria.

## Decision-making process

Technical decisions follow a collaborative process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an issue or RFC">
    Propose significant changes by filing a GitHub issue or RFC. Feature branches require an RFC and approval from at least two maintainers before they are created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community discussion">
    The community reviews the proposal in the issue thread or at the weekly meeting. Anyone can participate in the discussion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintainer review">
    Existing maintainers evaluate the proposal and reach consensus. The vLLM community holds final authority on decisions that affect the broader project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation and merge">
    Once approved, changes are implemented following the project's contribution guidelines and merged by a maintainer after passing CI and code review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Weekly community meeting

The vLLM Ascend community meets weekly to discuss ongoing work, proposals, and community updates.

- **When:** Every Wednesday, 15:00–16:00 UTC+8 ([convert to your timezone](https://dateful.com/convert/gmt8?t=15))
- **Agenda and notes:** [Meeting document](https://tinyurl.com/vllm-ascend-meeting)

All community members are welcome to attend and share ideas.

vLLM Ascend follows the vLLM community's code of conduct. All participants are expected to uphold these standards in every interaction — in GitHub issues, pull requests, meetings, and other community spaces.

## Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

All commits to vLLM Ascend must be signed off with a Developer Certificate of Origin. This certifies that you wrote or have the right to submit the code under the project's open-source license.

Sign off your commits using:

```bash
git commit -s -m "your commit message"
```

This adds a `Signed-off-by` line to your commit. Pull requests without signed-off commits will not be merged.
